*** Spiking NEAT
**** Introduction
***** What is Spiking NEAT?
      *Spiking NEAT* is an enhancement of the NEAT algorithm to
      leverage **Izhikevich Logistics** as the activation function. This
      will require several iterations to converge on the activation,
      and the resultant spike trains shall be encoded.

***** TODO Interfacing witth Spiking NEAT (Preliminary)
      + During the initial handshake, SN will send to
        the Dealers(s) the initial Population ID(s)
      + The Dealer will start streaming to SN.
      + SN (the Router) will receive this stream, and give it to the population(s) for
	result evaluations
      + SN, asynchronously, will send this result vector back to the Dealer
      + The dealer -- during the training phase -- will compute the error vectors
      + The Dealer will send the error vectors back to SN
      + Upon receiving the error vector, SN will evolve
        the population, along with a new population
        number.
      + The Delear will receive the updated population
        ID(s) and discard the old ones.

****** Multiple simultaneous populations?
      Since we will initially train on the crypto
      market, we will look at multiple time
      periods. Like 5-minute, 10-minute, 30-minute,
      1-hour, 1-day, etc. Why not have a different
      population for each? And we can allow
      cross-breeding among populations. A critter that
      does well in one time period is likely to also do
      well in an adjacent time period. So multiple
      result vectors will be sent back to the Dealer,
      one for each time period. Error vectors will then
      be sent to SN for evolution.
      + There is no need to send all of the population
        error vectors back right away, since what might
        be done on the 5-minute klines may not be
        complete on the 15-minute klines.
****** Seperate Eval Client?
      The Eval Client, while could be a part of the Data Client,
      is made a sperate module for scalabiliy purposes. Since
      the clients can be written in any language, the intent is to
      offer the greatest flexibility possible.
